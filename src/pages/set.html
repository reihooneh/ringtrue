Play a set

Two octaves of bars, each one designed by Ringtrue and drawn to scale. Tap a bar, or play from your computer keyboard. Change the wood and the whole set is redesigned.

Showing the built-in rosewood set, C4 to C6.

The keyboard

Keys A S D F G H J K L play the natural notes upward from the lowest C; W E T Y U play the sharps. Each bar is drawn at its real length, so you can see how much shorter the high notes are.
